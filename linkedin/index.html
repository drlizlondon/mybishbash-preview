Set up LinkedIn with myBishBash

When you open LinkedIn this way, myBishBash can appear first with the reminders, commitments and prompts you chose.

iPhone
  1. Open this page in Safari.
  2. Tap Share.
  3. Tap Add to Home Screen.
  4. Tap Add.
Android
  1. Open this page in Chrome.
  2. Tap the three dots.
  3. Tap Add to Home screen or Install app.
  4. Tap Add or Install.
